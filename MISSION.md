# Mission

H2H is a collection of overlays and dashboards for iRacing. It helps drivers see useful data while racing and stay focused on both the race and the information they need.

## Scope

H2H supports only iRacing. It presents information to the driver and does not control the car.

H2H is a support tool, not a replacement for the driver's judgement. The driver remains responsible for race strategy and decisions, including when information is
estimated or unavailable.

## Basis

The owner confirmed this purpose, scope, and driver responsibility during constitution initialization. See [README.md](README.md) for the implemented dashboards and
usage.
