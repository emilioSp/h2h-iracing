/ 01What it is

A pit wall on your screen.

Always know where you stand.

H2H puts four real-time overlays on your screen while you race. See the gap to the car ahead and behind, know if you're catching or losing time, and pit at the right lap.

Designed to be scannable in a glance, with the same colour language you already see on the broadcast. Read your race without taking your eyes off it.

Drop into SimHub or OBS.

Bundled SimHub dashboards are included in the download. Import them and you're done. Or paste each overlay's URL as a Browser Source in OBS for your stream.

No account, no cloud, no subscription. It all runs on your machine, and it's free and open source.

/ 02The overlays

Many overlays. One download.

01
Head-to-Head
See the cars ahead and behind in real time — live gap, delta per lap, and lap times for every battle.
02
Fuel
Know exactly how much fuel to add at your next pit stop, with zero, half and full-lap safety margins.
03
Weather
Air and track temperature, track wetness, plus a wind rose that orients itself to the car.
04
Car Telemetry
ABS, traction control, brake bias and pit limiter.

/ 04Setup

Up and running in under a minute.

01

Download the zip

Grab the latest release from GitHub. Unzip it anywhere on your Windows machine — no installer required.

02

Run h2h-iracing.exe

A welcome page opens in your browser with one-click links to every overlay, ready to use.

03

Add to SimHub or OBS

Import the bundled SimHub dashboards, or paste the overlay URLs as Browser Sources in OBS. Start iRacing and race.
