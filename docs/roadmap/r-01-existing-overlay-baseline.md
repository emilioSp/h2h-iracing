## Objective

Provide a baseline suite of iRacing overlays for use while driving. See the [overlay overview](../../README.md) and [architecture](../architecture.md).

## Completed When

The suite provides head-to-head, weather, car telemetry, fuel, spotter, and multiclass traffic overlays. Known fixes are tracked separately.
